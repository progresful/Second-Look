Scams don't wait in an inbox. They're on a laptop at checkout, on a paper letter, in a phone call. The moment that matters is right before you pay.

How it works

Just look. It reads, checks, and speaks.

01Your phone readsthe text on the device, twice a second. Nothing leaves yet.
02Only if it mattersone frame is checked with Gemini, in about two seconds.
03You hear whyone plain sentence, and a card on the glasses display.

Second Look boxes the message it's reading in yellow and marks each scam sign in red: a look-alike address, a price far below normal, a request for codes.

Clear answers

Not just a red flag. The reason.

Every check ends in one of four answers: likely scam, be careful, looks okay, or not sure. Each comes with what to do, why, and the exact signs it noticed. It never says something is “safe”.

24 kinds of scam signsPrices that don't add upLook-alike web addressesCodes & “safe accounts”
Call check

Listens to the call. Warns mid-call.

Turn it on for a call you're unsure about. It shows a live caption of what's said and speaks up the moment a caller asks for a code, a transfer to a “safe account” or remote access.

You choose

Guard what matters, on request or always on.

Letters & billsMessagesWebsitesWebshopsQR codesPhone calls

On request: tap check or press the glasses' capture button. Always on: it watches quietly and only speaks up if something looks wrong.

Warm voice, private by design

A calm voice that runs on your phone.

Answers are spoken in a warm voice (Kokoro-82M) generated on the iPhone itself, so it keeps talking offline. Card numbers, IBANs, personal IDs, phone numbers and emails are removed before any text leaves the phone. Nothing is stored.
