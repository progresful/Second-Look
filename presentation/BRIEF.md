## Form limits (ceilings)
- Name: 30 characters
- Punchline: 200 characters
- Description: 5,000 characters, markdown
- Presentation: PDF (or PPTX/PPT/ODP/KEY), up to 20 MB

## What this means for us
- All four criteria weigh the same, so the "trust" story (consent, no storage, honest "not sure") matters as much as the tech.
- Judges explicitly want robustness (permissions, errors, connectivity, device states). Our app has code paths for these, but most are **not verified on real glasses yet** (see REPORT.md).
